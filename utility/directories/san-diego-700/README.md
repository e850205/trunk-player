# San Diego City 700 MHz talkgroups

Talkgroup labels for the City of San Diego 700 MHz P25 system (RadioReference system 6610), built from the
RadioReference talkgroup listing on 2026-10-02. Police and school police talkgroups go to their department,
Fire/EMS and mutual aid to San Diego Fire-Rescue, lifeguards to San Diego Fire-Rescue Lifeguards and the rest to
the City of San Diego.

    ./manage.py import_directory --system 1 --talkgroups utility/directories/san-diego-700/talkgroups.csv --cities utility/directories/san-diego-700/cities.csv
